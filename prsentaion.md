# RestoMind Presentation Overview

## 1. Tagline

AI-powered bakery management platform that predicts demand, optimizes inventory, and reduces food waste through intelligent production planning.

---

## 2. The Problem

Small and medium-sized bakeries rely on manual experience to estimate daily production, often leading to overproduction or underproduction. Overproduction results in significant food waste and financial losses, while underproduction leads to missed sales opportunities and dissatisfied customers. Most existing solutions focus only on inventory or point-of-sale management and do not provide AI-driven demand forecasting, surplus detection, or actionable recommendations to optimize daily production decisions.

---

## 3. Your Solution

RestoMind is an AI-powered bakery management platform that combines demand forecasting, inventory management, and surplus detection in a single system. It analyzes historical sales and inventory data to predict daily production needs, recommends optimal production quantities, monitors inventory levels, detects surplus products before they become waste, and enables bakeries to sell excess products through an integrated marketplace. This data-driven approach helps bakery owners reduce food waste, optimize operations, and increase profitability.

---

## 4. Project Overview

RestoMind is an AI-powered bakery management platform designed to help bakery owners optimize daily operations and reduce food waste. It provides tools for inventory management, product and recipe management, demand forecasting, surplus detection, and AI-driven recommendations. Bakery managers use the platform to monitor inventory, plan daily production, and identify excess products, while customers can purchase discounted surplus items through an integrated marketplace. By combining operational management with artificial intelligence, RestoMind helps bakeries make smarter production decisions, reduce costs, and improve overall efficiency.

---

## 5. Key Features

- **AI Demand Forecasting** — Predicts daily product demand using historical sales data to optimize production planning.
- **Inventory Management** — Tracks ingredient quantities, batch information, and stock levels in real time.
- **Recipe Management** — Maintains standardized recipes and automatically links ingredients to bakery products.
- **Surplus Detection** — Identifies excess products before they become waste and generates actionable alerts.
- **AI Production Recommendations** — Recommends optimal daily production quantities to reduce overproduction and stock shortages.
- **Surplus Marketplace** — Allows bakeries to publish discounted surplus products for customers before they expire.
- **Business Dashboard** — Provides real-time insights into sales, inventory, waste, and operational performance through interactive analytics.
- **Role-Based Access Control** — Supports Admin, Manager, Staff, and Customer roles with secure, role-specific permissions.
- **AI Business Assistant** — Enables bakery managers to ask natural language questions about sales, inventory, and operations using an AI-powered chat assistant.
- **Waste Analytics & Reporting** — Generates detailed reports on food waste, demand trends, and production performance to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 6. Target Customer — Main Segment

Small and medium-sized bakery owners and managers who need a smarter way to plan daily production, manage inventory, and reduce food waste. RestoMind helps them make data-driven decisions through AI-powered demand forecasting, inventory optimization, surplus detection, and operational insights, ultimately improving profitability and efficiency.

## 7. Technical Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js (React)
- **Backend:** NestJS and business logic.
- **Database:** MongoDB
- **AI & Machine Learning:** Python-based machine learning models
- **Authentication & Security:** JWT authentication, role-based authorization (RBAC), rate limiting, CORS protection, Helmet security headers, and input validation.
- **Deployment & DevOps:** Docker for containerization, Vercel for deploying the Next.js frontend, AWS VPS for hosting the NestJS backend and AI services, GitHub for version control, and RESTful APIs for secure communication between the frontend, backend, and AI services.

---

## 8. Revenue Model

RestoMind operates as a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform with a recurring monthly subscription model. Restaurants and bakeries pay a fixed monthly fee to access the complete platform, including ERP, inventory management, AI demand prediction, production planning, AI recommendations, business analytics, AI assistant, and the surplus marketplace.

To accelerate adoption, new customers receive a one-month free pilot. The first 100 paying customers will be offered an early-adopter price, while the first 5–10 pilot bakeries that successfully complete the trial will receive a Founding Partner discount for their first year.

As the platform grows and AI usage increases, RestoMind plans to introduce usage-based AI credits and premium enterprise services as additional revenue streams.

---

## 9. Price Point (USD)

USD 20 per restaurant per month (introductory price for the first 100 customers), including full access to all platform features.

## 10. Your First 100 Customers

We will start by targeting tourist bakeries in Mansoura, where our team already has direct connections with several bakery owners. Instead of relying on online advertising, we will acquire our first customers through face-to-face visits, live product demonstrations, and one-month pilot deployments.

Our goal is to onboard 5–10 pilot bakeries, collect measurable results on waste reduction and operational efficiency, and use these case studies and customer referrals to expand across Mansoura before scaling to other cities in Egypt.

---

## 11. Competitors

RestoMind competes with two main solutions in the Egyptian market:

1. **Foodics** is a restaurant management platform that provides POS, inventory, and business management tools for restaurants and cafés.
2. **Mystery Bag** is an Egyptian surplus-food marketplace app. Restaurants, cafés, and other food businesses list their unsold/excess food at a discount, and customers browse and buy these "mystery bags" through the app before the food goes to waste. Businesses earn extra revenue on food they'd otherwise throw away, and customers get discounted meals — while cutting down on food waste overall.

This enables bakeries to optimize daily production, minimize food waste, and increase profitability through data-driven decision-making.

## 13. Does Your Project Use AI in Any Form?

Yes. RestoMind uses Artificial Intelligence as a core component of the platform. The platform also provides AI-powered production recommendations, surplus detection, and a natural language AI assistant that enables managers to query business data and receive actionable operational insights.
